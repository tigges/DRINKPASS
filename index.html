Units
Clock

Used for times in the list, chart and clinician summary. Time pickers follow your phone’s own clock setting. Exports always use 24-hour times.

After each log, ask for

Chosen options appear in the bar after each tap. Solid adds a Water, Solid, Both row after Pass, and Stool type adds the Bristol scale (1 to 7) for solids. Anything switched off stays under More. Notes and Change time are always under More.

Dots on the chart

By amount makes bigger drinks and passes show as bigger dots. It applies to 24 h, 3 d and 7 d.

Insights
Night hours

Tapping the moon (going to bed) and sun (up for the day) defines your night instead. These hours are the fallback when you haven’t marked sleep.

Drink reminders

A gentle prompt when you haven’t had a drink for a while, during the day only. It shows while the app is open or in the background; phones may not deliver it once the app is closed.

Storage

Your entries are saved in this browser, with automatic copies kept alongside. Clearing browser data removes both, so save a backup file now and then, for example to iCloud Drive or Files.

Automatic copies
Backup

With a password, the backup file is encrypted and can only be restored with that password. There is no way to recover a forgotten password.

To install: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome open the menu and choose Install app. Restore your backup from inside the installed app, because the browser tab and the installed app keep separate storage on iPhone.

Export everything

CSV opens in spreadsheet apps. FHIR is a standard health-data format that some record systems and apps can import. Both include every entry, not just recent days.
